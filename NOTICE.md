Re:Play Mod is a modified version of ReplayMod:

- Upstream source: https://github.com/ReplayMod/ReplayMod

official ReplayMod release and is not endorsed by the upstream project.

The full corresponding source must be made available under GPL-3.0-or-later whenever binaries are distributed.
